Canggu, Ubud & Beyond

Activities

🏄

Canggu Beach & Surf Scene

Batu Bolong and Berawa Beach — watch world-class surfers, swim in the Indian Ocean, rent a board if you're keen. The Canggu vibe is laid-back and cool.

Beach · Canggu
🎱

Finns Beach Club

Canggu's iconic beach club — day beds, pools, DJs, and cocktails. Book a day bed in advance. Also home to the Body Temple Spa if you want a combined beach + spa day.

Beach · Club · Canggu
🛕

Tirta Empul Temple

Sacred water purification ritual in natural spring pools. Arrive before 8 AM to participate. ~50k IDR entrance, sarong required.

Cultural · Must-Do
⛩

Gunung Kawi

Ancient royal tombs carved into a cliff wall above a river valley — one of Bali's most dramatic and underrated archaeological sites.

Cultural · Historical
🎭

Kecak Fire Dance

A chorus of 50+ men chanting around a fire at sunset — held at Uluwatu Temple or Ubud Palace. One of Bali's most unforgettable cultural experiences.

Cultural · Evening
🌅

Tanah Lot at Sunset

The most photographed temple in Bali — a sea temple on a rock formation offshore. Perfect en route from Canggu to Ubud on May 5. Arrive by 5 PM.

Cultural · Beach
🏺

Ubud Art Market

Handmade sarongs, wood carvings, silver jewelry, batik textiles. Great for gifts and souvenirs — bargaining is expected and part of the fun.

Cultural · Shopping
💍

Silver Jewelry Class

Make your own ring or pendant in ~2 hours. Traditional Balinese silversmithing — perfect activity for your GF on a tattoo day afternoon.

Cultural · Hands-On
🥾

Campuhan Ridge Walk

Free 2km sunrise walk through jungle and rice fields right on the edge of Ubud. Starts near Mount Lebah Temple. Beautiful at golden hour.

Jungle · Free
💧

Tukad Cepung Waterfall

An ethereal waterfall hidden inside a cave — shafts of sunlight pour in dramatically. One of the most magical spots near Ubud. Do this on May 9.

Jungle · Must-Do
🌿

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

UNESCO-listed rice terraces north of Ubud. Lunch spots with a view, Bali swings, photo ops. Best in morning light — go before 10 AM.

Jungle · Scenic
🐒

Sacred Monkey Forest

1,200+ macaques in a 14th-century temple jungle in Ubud town. Arrive at 9 AM. Don't bring food, bags, or loose glasses — the monkeys will steal them.

Jungle · Wildlife
🌋

Mount Batur Sunrise Hike

Active volcano with a stunning 2-hour sunrise hike. Depart Ubud at 4 AM with a guide. One of Bali's most rewarding day trips — best on a non-tattoo day.

Jungle · Adventure
👨‍🍳

Balinese Cooking Class

Learn to make nasi goreng, satay, and more in a local home kitchen. Often includes a morning market visit. Ends with eating everything you cooked.

Food · Experience
🍽

Locavore / Apéritif

Ubud's world-class fine dining. Locavore uses hyperlocal Indonesian ingredients; Apéritif has a colonial-era atmosphere with tasting menus. Reserve weeks ahead — perfect for your tattoo completion dinner.

Food · Fine Dining
🦐

Jimbaran Bay Seafood

Fresh grilled seafood eaten on the beach at sunset. Tables in the sand, candles, the whole thing. One of Bali's most iconic dining experiences.

Food · Beach
🍺

Canggu Café & Bar Scene

Canggu has Bali's best café culture — Old Man's Bar for sundowners, Shelter for smoothies, Da Maria for Italian. Great for your two days in the area.

Food · Canggu

Getting Around & Practical Info

Logistics

```
🗓

Hotel Breakdown

Two locations, two vibes

  • May 3–5: Courtyard by Marriott Bali Seminyak — Beach town, surf culture, great cafés. Laid-back arrival zone. Confirm early check-in since you arrive 12:10 AM May 4
  • May 5–10: Adiwana Alas Harum, Ubud — Cultural heart of Bali, jungle, temples, best base for tattoo days. Check in afternoon of May 5
  • Ask Canggu hotel for driver recommendation for the Canggu → Ubud transfer on May 5
  • Ask Adiwana Alas Harum about late checkout or luggage storage on May 10 departure
🚗

Private Driver

Recommended for all ground transport

Book Soon
  • Pre-book airport transfer: DPS → Canggu hotel (~45 min, ~$25–35 USD)
  • Full-day driver ~$40–70 USD for up to 10 hours — great value
  • Ubud → DPS on May 10: ~1.5–2 hrs, book the day before
  • Book via hotel concierge, Klook, GetYourGuide, or ask on arrival
  • Negotiate a multi-day rate with the same driver — saves ~15%
  • WhatsApp is how all drivers communicate — get their number immediately
📡

Connectivity

eSIM or local SIM

  • Buy an eSIM before departure — Airalo, Holafly, or Maya work great for Indonesia
  • Or grab a Telkomsel SIM at DPS airport on arrival (fast, cheap data)
  • WhatsApp is used for everything in Bali — driver, spa, restaurant bookings
  • Most hotels, cafes, and spas have solid WiFi
💵

Money & Currency

Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)

  • ~1 USD ≈ 16,000 IDR — everything feels pleasantly cheap
  • Visa on Arrival: ~$35 USD — have cash or card ready at DPS immigration
  • Withdraw IDR from ATMs in Ubud town center (avoid airport exchange rates)
  • Most restaurants and shops accept card; spas and drivers may prefer cash
  • Budget ~$150–250 USD/day for two (mid-range comfortable travel)
🎨

Tattoo Prep

Before & during

  • Confirm artist booking — get studio address, WhatsApp, and start time
  • Double-check coordinates on forearm design: 22.9068°S, 43.1729°W
  • Eat a big meal before each session — 6–10 hrs in the chair
  • Pack: snacks, Pocari Sweat, headphones, phone stand, portable charger
  • Wear tank top or loose short-sleeve on both tattoo days
  • Ask artist about Saniderm / second-skin wrap — ideal for traveling with fresh ink
  • After sessions: no sun, no swimming on that arm, loose fabric only
🌡️

Health & Safety

May conditions & tips

  • May = early dry season — hot (~30°C/86°F), low humidity, minimal rain
  • Reef-safe sunscreen (required at some Canggu beaches)
  • DEET mosquito repellent — dengue risk, especially near jungle/rice fields
  • Travel insurance strongly recommended — World Nomads or MedJet
  • Drink bottled or filtered water only — widely available and cheap
  • Temple dress codes: shoulders and knees covered (sarong usually provided)
```
